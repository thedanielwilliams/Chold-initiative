Partnership pathways

Six ways organisations work with us.

Government collaboration

Technical assistance to federal, state and LGA authorities on livestock policy implementation, data management and inter-agency coordination.

Public-private partnerships

Structuring PPPs for animal health infrastructure, laboratories, cold chain and value chain services.

Donor-funded projects

Delivery of technical scopes for multilateral, bilateral and philanthropic funders, with M&E built in from baseline.

Academic research networks

Joint applied and participatory research on animal health, genetics, feed systems and climate adaptation.

Technical collaborations

Consortium delivery with veterinary bodies, technology providers and regional animal health centres.

Grassroots-led initiatives

Community and cooperative programmes convened through traditional institutions and producer associations.
